Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions

Do participants need any coding experience?

Absolutely not. Our sessions are designed for people who've never written a line of code. CEOs, PMs, researchers — everyone builds. The AI tools we use mean you describe what you want in plain English and watch it come to life.

Can we run the workshop remotely?

Yes. We run sessions both in-person and remotely via video call with screen sharing. Remote sessions work brilliantly — the facilitator can jump between participants' screens seamlessly. In-person sessions add the energy of being in a room together.

What AI tools do you use in the workshop?

Primarily Claude Code and similar AI coding assistants. We focus on tools that let non-technical people build real, functional prototypes through natural language. We'll recommend the best tools for your specific use cases during the discovery call.

How far in advance do we need to book?

We recommend 2–3 weeks minimum to allow for the discovery call and pre-workshop setup. However, we can accommodate urgent requests with as little as one week's notice, subject to availability.

What about data security and NDAs?

We take security seriously. We're happy to sign NDAs, work within your data handling policies, and can structure sessions to avoid exposing sensitive code or data. For regulated industries, we offer on-premise options.

Can we get an invoice and pay by bank transfer?

Of course. We issue proper VAT invoices and accept bank transfer (BACS), card payments, and purchase orders for enterprise clients. Payment terms are typically net 14 days.
